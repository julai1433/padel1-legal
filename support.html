How do I run a tournament?
Add your players from the home screen, start a tournament, and the app builds the rounds and matchups for you. Enter each match's score as it finishes and the app keeps standings, points, and the bracket up to date automatically.
Is there a subscription or ads?
No subscription and no ads. There's an optional one-time Pro purchase that unlocks extra features — everything else in the app is free to use.
How does syncing between phones work?
During a live tournament you can connect nearby iPhones over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth so scores stay in sync across devices without retyping them. It's entirely local — device to device, no internet connection needed and no server involved. If you don't need it, you can run the whole tournament on a single phone instead.
Two devices aren't finding each other
Make sure both iPhones have Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on, are unlocked and nearby, and that you've allowed Reina de la Pista's local network permission when iOS asked for it (check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network if you're not sure). Being on the same Wi-Fi network usually helps devices find each other faster.
Why does the app ask for Health access?
If you'd like your padel sessions to count toward your Apple Health activity and to see workout stats (calories, active minutes) alongside your tournament results, grant Health access when prompted. It's entirely optional — decline it and the app works the same, just without those stats.
Can I use it on my Apple Watch?
Yes — the Watch app mirrors live scores and round timing from your iPhone during a match.
I found a bug or lost some data
Email us with what happened and, if you can, what you were doing right before it — device model and iOS version help too. Tournament data lives only on your device, so if you've deleted and reinstalled the app, past history won't come back.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. Reina de la Pista works fully offline for running a tournament on one device. An internet connection is only ever used by Apple itself, for the optional Pro purchase or for HealthKit/iCloud syncing you've set up at the system level.
